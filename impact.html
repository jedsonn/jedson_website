○ Scope and Method

A public-web audit through August 16, 2026 identified 22 verified placements representing 17 distinct narratives. All 22 are listed above, each labelled by type. Policy and regulatory citations are counted separately from media coverage, author-written translations are distinguished from independent reporting, and syndicated republications of a single item are counted as reach rather than as separate stories. Item 5, the FCA comment letter, is author-initiated engagement rather than coverage, and is not part of the 22.

Eleven further leads were tested and not counted: unverified claims with no recoverable source, a citation that proved to refer to a different paper, and searches of other regulators that returned no exact match. The audit file records each one and the reason.
